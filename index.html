About

I am a frontend developer and Information Systems student in The Gambia, building web tools that people actually use.

Education

Third-year BSc Information Systems at the University of The Gambia, School of Information Technology and Communication. I have studied Java and object-oriented design in depth, alongside cloud computing, access control and systems analysis.

How I build

I started web development from scratch in January 2026 at Jasseh Code Camp, then kept going with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Node and Express. I ship small, finish what I start, and learn from feedback.

Leadership

I led BETECHIFIED, a ten-person frontend team across The Gambia, Nigeria, Ghana and Sierra Leone. My hackathon team took first place at UTG-ITCA Hackathon 1.0, where I handled testing, bug fixing, naming, design and branding.

What is next

A career in software engineering, and postgraduate study abroad. Until then I keep building, one project at a time.
